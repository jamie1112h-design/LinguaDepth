About Language Learning Levels

CEFR levels describe practical can-do ability across reading, listening, speaking, and writing – not a count of memorised words or rules. This is self-assessed: ask yourself "Can I introduce myself and ask basic questions?" (likely A1), "Can I handle familiar situations without much help?" (likely B1), or "Can I discuss complex subjects and understand demanding texts?" (likely B2–C1). It isn't a formal placement test. If your skills are uneven – say, stronger reading than speaking – pick the level matching your weaker skill, unless a future version lets you set levels per skill.
