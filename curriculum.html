Mockup notes for review, not shipped copy:
1. Module 1's six lesson titles and Module 2–4's names/blurbs are content proposals from me, not yet confirmed by you – the "street conversations first" ordering is your instruction; the specific topics inside it are my draft.
2. Real architecture question this raises: PrecisionSteps generates one free-text subject per session with no memory between visits. A curriculum needs the opposite – a fixed sequence that persists across a learner's visits and tracks which module/lesson they've completed. That's a genuine new piece (a `curriculum` and `progress` table in Supabase, keyed to the access code), not something the existing engine does today – worth its own decision before backend work starts.
3. Open question I haven't resolved: is the six-lesson Module 1 the same fixed curriculum for every learner of a given target language (authored or generated once, then reused), or generated fresh per learner? Fixed-and-shared is cheaper and lets you hand-review the crucial first module; generated-per-learner costs more and is harder to quality-control for exactly the module meant to hook people.
4. Locked-module blurbs are deliberately visible (not hidden behind a "???") – seeing what's coming is itself part of the enticement; only the lesson-by-lesson breakdown is hidden until unlocked.
5. Fonts corrected to the platform-wide Georgia + Arial pairing (Sitrep v1.35/v1.36, 2026-08-16), matching the same fix on the intro screen.
6. The course title now reads from the URL (?target=fr-QC etc.), passed by the intro page's "Begin With a Pre-Built Curriculum" button – open this file directly with no parameters and it falls back to "a language" rather than guessing.
